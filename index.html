05 / REFLECTION

Evidence matters.
So does what I learn from it.

Reflection should explain what you personally did, what the artefact demonstrates, what challenged you and how you would improve next time.

✦

Important: Reflection prompts are not a substitute for your own experience. Edit the draft areas with truthful details before submitting.

01
Team charterPurpose, agreements and collaboration
+

A team charter helps establish a shared understanding of the project’s purpose, responsibilities and ways of working. The Trello history shows that a project-charter task was created and its checklist was marked complete. The actual charter document still needs to be attached to this portfolio.

Personalise this reflection: Add what you contributed to the charter, one team agreement that mattered in practice, and what you would change about how the team agreed on responsibilities.

02
User stories & tasksConnecting features to user needs
+

The Sprint 1 registration story is written from the customer’s perspective and is broken into interface design, form implementation and validation tasks. This illustrates how a user need can be translated into smaller pieces of work.

Personalise this reflection: Describe the story or task you personally worked on, how you checked whether it met the user need, and what you learned from splitting work into smaller tasks.

03
Product backlogKeeping the product’s work visible
+

The PowerShare board includes work relating to customer accounts, battery discovery, bookings, payments, rental management, inventory and administration. Keeping these items visible helps the team see the range of work required.

Personalise this reflection: Explain how your team decided what mattered most, give one example of a priority decision, and state how you would make backlog priorities clearer.

04
Sprint backlogPlanning a manageable increment
+

The board groups work into sprint-labelled stories and tasks. The export records completed checklist items for Sprints 1–6, while Sprint 7’s administration tasks remain incomplete. This makes the difference between planned work and recorded checklist status visible.

Personalise this reflection: Add your actual sprint responsibility, whether the team achieved its sprint goal, and what you would change about estimating or tracking work.

05
Agile logbookLearning from the process
+

Dated Trello activity provides a partial timeline of changes, but it is not the same as a complete Agile logbook. A logbook should capture what happened, blockers, decisions, learning and follow-up actions.

Personalise this reflection: Add an example from your logbook, a real blocker or decision, how the team responded, and one action you would carry into the next sprint.
