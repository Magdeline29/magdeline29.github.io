## Add before submission
Upload or screenshot your real logbook and select dated entries that demonstrate progress, blockers, decisions, daily updates and retrospective actions. Remove private information that should not be public.

A useful entry format, if your lecturer permits it:

| Date | Work planned / completed | Blocker or decision | Learning / next action |
|---|---|---|---|
| Add actual date | Describe actual work | Record actual issue or decision | State actual learning and follow-up |

Do not fill this table with invented events. Use your original logbook.

## Reflection prompts — replace with your own experience

- How did I or the team respond to the issue?
- What did I learn, and what specific action would I take in a future sprint?
