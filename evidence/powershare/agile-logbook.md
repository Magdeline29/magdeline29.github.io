## Dated progress entries

### 14 July 2026
The entry records team work refining the business idea and defining the problem experienced by micro-entrepreneurs in Khayelitsha and Mitchells Plain. It identifies barbers, tailors and spaza shops as examples of businesses affected by outages and records review of market-validation survey information. Planned work was to analyse survey findings and refine the business model and value proposition. The stated challenge was connecting survey findings clearly to the business problem.

### 15 July 2026
The entry records analysis of the market-validation survey. It says the findings indicated financial losses during outages, limited access to backup power and interest in an affordable rental solution. Planned work was to finalise the operational workflow: charging stations at a central hub, delivery to local businesses, collection after the outage and recharging. The entry flags the need to present the proposed R50–R80 daily rental price consistently.

### 16 July 2026
The entry records finalising the value proposition and identifying target customers, including barbers, tailors, spaza shops and internet cafés. It notes that one group member was unavailable and that tasks were redistributed among the remaining members. Planned work was to complete and review the presentation content.

### 17 July 2026
The entry records a final review of the business model and presentation, checking the problem statement, survey results, market opportunity, workflow, value proposition and target customers for consistency. It also records reviewing the presentation design and communicating the community benefit of reducing entrepreneurs' income loss. The next step was preparing final deliverables and making final corrections.
