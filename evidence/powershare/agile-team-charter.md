## Verified source document

## Project purpose and vision
The charter describes Power-Share as an affordable portable-power rental service for micro-entrepreneurs affected by load shedding. Its objective is to help small businesses continue operating during power outages and reduce income loss. Its vision is to empower township entrepreneurs with reliable, affordable and accessible backup power.

## Values, communication and working agreements
The charter lists collaboration, respect, accountability and transparency as core values. It identifies WhatsApp for daily updates and coordination, weekly in-person campus meetings, and shared Google Docs or Word documents. It asks members to meet deadlines, communicate early, respect meeting times and contribute actively. Conflict is to be discussed openly, with the team leader mediating unresolved issues and escalation to the lecturer if necessary.

## Goals and milestones
The charter sets SMART goals, including MVP validation within one to two weeks of the testing phase, with a target of at least 20 WhatsApp group members and 5–10 pre-orders. Its milestones move from project idea and research, through marketing strategy, MVP design and setup, customer outreach, feedback and improvement, to final submission and presentation.
