## Add before submission
1. Add a screenshot or PDF of the actual charter (only share content approved for public viewing).
2. Identify the team purpose, scope, roles, values, communication expectations and working agreements using the charter's actual wording.
3. Explain your own contribution and point to the relevant section of the artefact.

## Reflection prompts — replace with your own experience
- What did I personally contribute to creating or reviewing the charter?
- Which agreement helped the team work together, and what is my evidence for that?
- What would I improve about agreeing on responsibilities or communication next time?
